About

I work on 3D perception for robots in environments that change: people walk past, objects are moved, and a map built a minute ago is already partly wrong. My goal is scene representations in which every moving object is tracked, reconstructed over time, and recognised when it comes back.

I came to computer vision from mathematics, with a B.Sc. from the University of Delhi and an M.Sc. from IIT Madras. I then worked as a research assistant at Academia Sinica on deep learning for 3D microscopy and animal tracking, before starting my M.S. in EECS at NYCU in 2026. I am looking for PhD positions in robot perception, 3D vision and human-robot interaction.
